Teacher Answer Key & Scoring Guide

Exemplar Responses & Criteria for Flight WR-12

1 Stage 1: Prewriting (3-2-1 Matrix Exemplar)

Topic: Should Artificial Intelligence be Integrated into High School Classrooms?

3 Core Ideas: (1) Personalized adaptive tutoring for struggling writers, (2) Immediate draft feedback, (3) Development of critical AI literacy for future careers.

2 Logical Reasons: (1) High school educators lack the time to provide paragraph-by-paragraph live feedback daily, (2) Modern employers require ethical prompt navigation.

1 Concrete Example: The 2024 Khanmigo school pilot demonstrated a 34% increase in student thesis clarity through Socratic AI questioning.

2 Stage 2: Drafting (Rough First Version)

Accept rough phrasing, colloquial fragments, and spelling lapses. The goal is momentum, not polish.

"AI in high school is actually a super helpful tool when its used correctly. First, lots of students struggle with writing essays because teachers are busy. AI can give fast feedback. Also it teaches kids how to use prompt tech. Like Khanmigo showed students became way more confident when practicing with an AI coach."

3 Stage 3: Revising (Structural & Logical Upgrade)

Key Revisions Made:

  • Replaced vague opener ("super helpful tool") with an assertive thesis claim.
  • Added formal transition signposts: "Primarily," "Furthermore," "Consequently."
  • Integrated the quantitative metric (34% growth metric) to validate assertion.

4 Stage 4: Editing Key (Error Hunter Solutions)

1. Subject-Verb: "utilizes" → "utilize" (plural subject: students)
2. Spelling: "flurish" → "flourish"
3. Capitalization: "mr. henderson" → "Mr. Henderson"
4. Punctuation: "?" → "," (comma) for dependent clause

5 Stage 5: Publishing & Oral Presentation Rubric

Criteria Proficient (3 pts) Advanced (4 pts)
Clarity & Voice Audible, steady pacing, occasional notes check. Dynamic projection, confident cadence, commands room.
Rhetorical Content Clear thesis with supporting reasons. Compelling thesis backed by nuanced evidence & rebuttal.
